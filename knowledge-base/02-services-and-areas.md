# Services, fuels, brands and areas

## Services
Boiler installation and replacement. Boiler repairs. Boiler servicing. Central heating.
Gas safety checks and landlord gas safety certificates. Power flushing. Air conditioning,
domestic and commercial. Heat pump installation. General plumbing and bathroom installations.

No boiler cover plan and no service plan are offered.

## Fuels
Gas and LPG accepted. Oil is not covered.

## Brands
Boilers: Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Glow-worm, Baxi.
Air conditioning: Worcester Bosch, Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Samsung.

## Areas
Billericay, Basildon, Wickford, Rayleigh, Chelmsford, Brentwood, Stanford-le-Hope, Benfleet,
Southend-on-Sea, Canvey Island, Rochford, Grays, Rainham, Romford, Dagenham.
