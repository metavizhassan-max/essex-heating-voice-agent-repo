# Price, finance and warranty

## New boiler
Starts from £2,160 including VAT. The real price depends on the job and the location.
It is a rough price, not a fixed price.

## Repairs and servicing
No published prices. The team confirms the cost, and nothing is done without the customer
agreeing it first.

## Charges
The home survey is free. There are no call-out charges.

## Finance
0% finance on Worcester Bosch boilers and on air conditioning only. No deposit required,
subject to status and a credit check.

## Warranty
Manufacturer warranty of 5 to 12 years on parts and labour, depending on the boiler.
5 year workmanship guarantee on the company's own work.
